To Whom It May Concern,

I am writing to ask whether your organization can assist me with what I believe is disability discrimination by my employer. I have worked as a warehouse inventory clerk at a distribution center in Glenmara for almost six years. In November I was diagnosed with a chronic condition that, on some days, requires me to take short breaks to manage symptoms. My doctor provided a letter recommending two additional ten-minute breaks per shift.

I submitted the letter to my supervisor and to human resources. Instead of discussing it, my supervisor began writing me up for "leaving my station," including on days I had used the breaks my doctor recommended. Within two months I went from having a clean record to having three written warnings, and last week I was told I am being moved to on-call status with no guaranteed hours.

I have kept everything: the doctor's letter, copies of the write-ups, my schedule before and after I disclosed my condition, and an email from HR acknowledging they received the accommodation request. Coworkers have offered to confirm that I have always completed my assigned work.

I do not know whether I have a case, but I cannot afford to lose this job and I do not think I am being treated fairly. I would appreciate the chance to speak with someone. Email is the easiest way to reach me during the day.
